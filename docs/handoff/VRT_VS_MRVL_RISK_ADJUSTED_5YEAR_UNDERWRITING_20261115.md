## Bottom line for user
**Choose VRT now on risk-adjusted investment evidence** if only one position is being studied: larger proven GAAP EBIT margins and realized operating/owner cash conversion, integrated cooling+power/service execution, current positive FY2026 FCF guidance. **MRVL has potentially bigger long-run fundamental upside and stronger optical silicon IP moat**, supported by Gartner July 2026 Company to Beat for optical AI DC connectivity, but enormous pending LITE equity acquisition and Google's performance-based warrants mean 2027–2031 per-share returns cannot be judged by MRVL FY2031 sales projection or undiluted EPS. **VRT itself is not cheap; this is a relative preference not an unconditional buy.** Optical/semiconductor market may grow faster, but investment return measured in *price paid per share* vs share-specific FCF/distributions.

**MRVL six months ended Aug 1 2026:** GAAP CFO **$1,244.3M**, capex **$282.4M** → simple FCF proxy **$961.9M**. MRVL FY27Q2 gross margin 53.1% and GAAP op ~16.8%; VRT has lower product gross margin but higher currently realized GAAP operating margin. Need future GAAP owner FCF per *fully diluted share* and return on incremental capital, not leverage solely P/E or PSR.

## Common-denominator valuation: TARGET shareowner FCF
The **same fair-valuation output** for both is **normalized fully diluted free cash flow per share** at a chosen date, multiple and risk sensitivity, factoring SBC, acquisitions, maintenance/growth capex, signed customer programs, debt and share buybacks. Inputs **not** identical: VRT cooling project delivery/backlog/service, MRVL design-win timing/custom ASIC volumes/optical adoption/partner manufacturing.
- Compare 2027-31 incremental return on capital, 5yr probability-weighted cash generation, terminal shareholder FCF yield and EV / normalized NOPAT/FCF rather than comparing raw PSR.
- VRT 2026 guided adj EBIT midpoint $3.515B, EV/issuer-adj EBIT **$77.45B/$3.515B=22.0x**; 2026 adj FCF $2.7B, mcap yield 3.47%; 2026 adjusted EPS midpoint $7.15, P/issuer-adj EPS 28.3x. FY2026 GAAP EPS guidance $6.29 => P/GAAP forward 32.1x. All nonGAAP explicitly labeled.

### 5yr illustrative shared hurdle, not forecast / price target
**Question:** What **2031 cash flow** must each stock support so an investor at Nov13 price earns exactly **15% annual stock price return over five years**, assuming market grants BOTH an identical **25x equity P/owner FCF** in Nov2031, zero dividend/buybacks/FX and 2031 terminal net debt not separately modeled? This is a mechanical stress test. 1.15^5 ≈ 2.01136.
- VRT 2026 Nov mcap $77.81B → 2031 mcap **$156.5B**, 2031 FCF required **$6.26B/year**. Compared to FY2026 issuer-adjusted FCF $2.7B midpoint, requires **~18.3% annual adjusted-to-owner-FCF compounding** over five years. This is a *nonGAAP start to economic owner target* heuristic (not formally like-for-like).
- MRVL 2026 Nov current standalone mcap $137.63B, **if signed merger closes on stated ~67% legacy shareholder stake** and no extra warrants, share-equivalent **pro forma equity mcap ~$205.4B** at current share price. To double pre-existing MRVL share price through 2031, combined equity mcap **~$413.1B** and FCF required **~$16.52B/yr** (25x). This is NOT a forecast; ignores postclose share issuance beyond merger, LITE current market enterprise debt/cash changes, Google warrants, multiple conditions, cash acquired; implied FCF target ~2.4× issuer's Nov 2026 annualized hypothetical **nonGAAP combined operating income ~$6.9B**, even before tax, so a very substantial continuation of growth / integration is demanded. Company's Oct FY31 standalone sales goal $70-90B could support high FCF if actual, but target is unconfirmed and not guaranteed revenue. **Do not compare MRVL target mechanically to preclose standalone FCF; two different perimeters**.
- The same terminal 25x is *not necessarily economically correct*: P/FCF paid at exit should reflect 2031 growth and duration, interest rates, competitive IP, sustainability. A 15x or 35x terminal would change outcomes greatly. These estimates explicitly exclude equity dilution in Google warrant and no acquisitions beyond LITE.
- VRT has meaningful multiple compression risk despite profitability. MRVL has 2026 valuation/high multiples, much higher M&A and GOOGL contingent warrant uncertainty, plus potential higher upside if full optics+custom silicon roadmap delivers.
