# Stock PM — Can emerging-market industrialization sustain 10% JBTM organic growth?
**As of 2026-11-15 KST**, completed research, no trade authorized. Research universe **JBTM / PAHC only**; JBTM R1 remains next main task. Source URLs near foot.

## 1. Executive verdict
- Emerging-market poultry processing industrialization, rising throughput, scaling with low staffing, poultry/seafood relative protein shift, and product SKU value-added cutting/packing are coherent long-duration tailwinds, **but they do NOT make sustained JBTM companywide 10% organic sales CAGR the central case**.
- **PM modeled 2027–2035 company organic sales CAGR:** conservative ~2.5%, base ~5.1%, strong ~7.6%, ultra-strong ~10.0% requiring Protein Solutions ~14.46% organic annually for nine years while Prepared/Food/Beverage +4%. These are *assumptions and inverse conditions*, not externally estimated likelihood/projections.
- Trailing 12m GAAP operating margin through 2026-09-30 ~7.3% ($286.3M / $3,919M), standalone 2026Q3 **10.1%** ($100M/$994M). FY2026 guided **adjusted EBITDA margin 17.25–17.50%**, 2028 targeted **20.0%** (NOT GAAP operating margin), corresponding at constant Q3 non-GAAP gap ~7.9pp to *illustrative* GAAP ~12.1% if same difference were to persist. Difference does **not** stay stable by default. PM sustainable normalized GAAP EBIT margin base ~10–12% medium-term, potentially ~11–13% longer-term, optimistic 13–14% if aftermarkets/digital and cost savings strengthen. Not verified company target.
- The old **2014–2024 ~10.46% CAGR** is JBT FoodTech **reported** sales growth with acquisitions and FY2018 ASC606 effects, not an organic combined JBTM CAGR and cannot be projected independently. 2026 9mo actual **+4.3% reported, +1.8% organic**, with favorable FX +2.5pp; FY2026 guided ~$3.95–4.00B sales (~+4.0–5.3% vs 2025). 2026 starting midpoint **$3.975B**.

## 2. Why emerging markets may adopt automation despite low wages
- Customer ROI = higher edible meat/fish yield & sellable product mix; throughput/yield; reduced staffing; fewer defects and waste; sanitation/food safety/traceability; shift uptime; later maintenance — **not just wage savings**.
- Industrial plant **scale economies**: reliable affordable poultry supply and 2-shift high utilization amortize a large CAPEX project over much larger quantity. Servicing many plants and having local parts support can create supplier scale as well. Miniature plants with intermittent supply/downtime and high interest may be economically unsuitable for expensive sophisticated machines.
- Hypothetical/NOT JBT customer disclosure: 100 tonnes/day raw poultry, 300 days/year; +0.5 **percentage point** edible yield → +150 tonnes/year at **$2/kg contribution margin** → $300,000/year; 10 staffing FTE × **$8,000 annual fully loaded cost** → $80,000/year; annual incremental maintenance/energy $80,000; net $300,000/year; $2M all-in system CAPEX gives **6.67-year simple payback** before finance, tax, implementation disruption, yield variance. Without verified yield gains, payback becomes economically unattractive. With better verified line yield/throughput and retention of product value, return improves. These are *invented* illustrative economics, not observed JBT results.
- Company verified order synergies: e.g. Indonesia $3M additional processing line, Brazil $4M prepared foods integration and $1M portioning, Poland $5M nuggets, Australia $4M greenfield, US poultry $3M secondary processing. JBT says LATAM/APAC expected to outgrow other areas and 200K+ installed machines/service aftermarket capture estimated ~40%→50%+ target. Commercial case is real but **not independent audited customer ROI**, and growth target is not proof.

## 3. Why poultry/fish transition alone insufficient
- OECD–FAO 2026–35 baseline 2035 global poultry consumption **+20%**, beef consumption **+8%**, pig consumption **+4%** vs 2023–25, world aquatic food consumption **+12%**, total farmed aquaculture production **+18%** (different denominators). Climate pressure makes some *relative* beef-to-poultry switching plausible, but no guaranteed absolute worldwide beef/pork consumption declines; pigs have considerably less enteric methane than ruminant cattle. Some fish farm production is shellfish/prawns or species not addressed profitably by JBTM's portfolio.
- JBTM 2025 equipment order end-market mix **44% poultry, 6% seafood, 16% meat, remaining 34% others**. These percentages are **equipment orders** NOT sales; beef/pig shifting to poultry benefits one product line and can weaken another.
- Material growth beyond protein *physical kilograms* depends on formalization of plants, rapid CAPEX automation penetration, higher processed-product value/SKUs, integrated lines, product standardization, better equipment price capture and aftermarkets. **No guaranteed multiplier** of food volume to equipment spending, especially when capital is dear or local competitors offer cheaper solutions.
- OECD baseline demand rises slowly (~1–2%/y in poultry/fish volume); company Investor Day 2025–28 **5–7% organic CAGR target** assumes 3–4pp market processing equipment/technology spend, ~1pp revenue synergies and 1–2pp initiatives. 2026 9mo organic only 1.8%: current bookings/market share are key catalysts, not long-run macro trends alone.

## 4. Segment-based organic sales scenarios through 2035
Base FY2026 **$3.975B** consolidated revenue (company guidance midpoint); most recent Jan–Sep 2026 segment revenue ratio **Protein Solutions $1.396B / $2.911B = 47.955%** and Prepared Food/Beverage **52.045%**. **Assume these segment mix weights apply approximately to full year FY2026**, no FX, no M&A, no divestiture; both segment revenues compound at specified nominal USD annual organic growth rates for **nine years (2027–2035)**.

Math:
`Revenue2035 = 3.975 * [0.47956*(1+gProtein)^9 + 0.52044*(1+gPrepared)^9] USD billion`.
`CompanyCAGR = (Revenue2035/3.975)^(1/9)-1`.
`EBIT2035 = Revenue2035 × assumed *GAAP operating profit margin*, not adjusted EBITDA.`

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Downside | 3% | 2% | **2.49%** | **$4.960B** | 9% | **$0.446B** |
| **Base** | **7%** | **3%** | **5.07%** | **$6.204B** | **11%** | **$0.682B** |

**No statistical confidence intervals**: protein 7% and Prepared 3% long-run rates *chosen* for base; extrapolating customer CAPEX and market shares is conjectural. The "ultra strong" 14.46% Protein CAGR is **solved backward** to force consolidated 10% sales CAGR for 9 years and is **not a predicted outcome**. 2028 (2y after FY2026) margin synergy one-off ramp can support growth; 2029–2035 sustained high growth must come from actual revenue and capital efficiency.
**Avoid double counting** company 2025–28 growth bridge synergy +1% if already reflected inside 7% Protein or 3% Prepared. No assumed acquisition effect. Market 2025 equipment poultry/seafood 44%/6% are NOT segment-revenue weights.

## 5. Sustainable operating margin — what is actual vs what is planned
- **2026 GAAP:** TTM ending September 2026 $286.3M operating income / $3.919B revenue **7.3%**; standalone Q3 $100M/$994M **10.1%**. TTM includes merger-related amortization/restructuring, some impairment costs, and Q2 GAAP margin only 4.7%. Q3 standalone cannot be treated as a stable run rate without follow-through.
- 2026 GAAP 9mo 7.4% operating margin (2025 9mo 4.2%). FY2026 company guided 17.25–17.50% **adjusted EBITDA**; 2028 20% **adjusted EBITDA**. Company did not guide a sustainable GAAP operating margin of 20%.
- March 2026 Investor Day 2025 adjusted EBITDA 15.8% → 2028 20% via **organic revenue growth/pricing +110–130bp**, **Marel cost synergies +225–250bp**, **continuous improvement +50–75bp** (minor rounding). $43M real 2025 savings; company estimated exit 2025 $85M **run-rate**; 2027 year-end ~$150M **run-rate** (this $150M is total, NOT incremental on top of $85M). 2028 margin 20% depends on risks and successful initiatives.
- GAAP operating margin PM planning ranges (NOT company guidance): through 2028 **10–12% base** given FY2026 Q3 achieved 10.1%, 2035 **11–13% durable base-to-good** if recurring aftermarket/digital share, installed fleet, premium yield technologies and cost design offset pricing competitive pressure, intangible amortization, and customer/region mix. Downside <9% if merger amortization/impairments/operational delays persist. 14% *stretch* requires stronger evidence of product-level contribution margin, software recurring gross margin and reduced amortization/cost adjustment.
- JBTM 2026 Q3 adjusted EBITDA 18.0% **vs GAAP operating margin 10.1% (7.9pp gap)**; 2026 non-GAAP EPS guidance $8.10–8.40 vs GAAP EPS $4.30–4.60. Company 2026 FY adjustments include acquisition-related amortization/depreciation approx **$3.23/share** plus restructuring and M&A expense, impairment. **No shortcut** from 20% adjusted EBITDA 2028 to 20% EBIT.
- Company's recurring share about 50% includes **maintenance, spare parts, rebuilds, leasing, subscription/software**, not 50% AI SaaS. Products integrated with AI/machine vision, but companywide AI pricing power not established.
- The margin consequences of faster fish business growth **may be negative** in short run as fish unit economics are less mature; faster fish sales do not guarantee companywide margin expansion.
- For stock price, EPS/FCF per share, debt and dilution/repurchases, invested capital and WACC and valuation multiples matter, not simply EBIT margin rising.

## 6. Monitoring tests required before revising current 4–6% base toward 10% organic
1. Measured >6–8% organic **orders** for at least ~4 quarters (not reported FX growth), sustained backlog conversion and food-product category mix; FY2026 +1.8% Jan–Sep organic baseline.
2. Disclosed regional (APAC, Latin America, Africa) pipeline/orders per processor, line utilization/payback and post-install actual profit contribution vs local equipment competitors.
3. Food services installed fleet >200K machines and aftermarket capture ~40% target 50%+ realized, independent retention/price/cost and software billings.
4. Organic Protein Solutions 10–15% for multiple years with Prepared/Food/Beverage healthy >3–4%; seafood margins and real share across species.
5. FY2028 company $150M cumulative annualized synergy reached with repeatable GAAP EBIT margin 10–12%, and post-2028 incremental margin maintained with ROIC above WACC.
6. Verify EBIT→FCF after capex and acquisition expenses, and compare valuation per share, not historical reported sales CAGR alone.
