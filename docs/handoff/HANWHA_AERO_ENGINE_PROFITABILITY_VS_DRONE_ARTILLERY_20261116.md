# Hanwha Aerospace — Independent aero-engine IP as long-run profit pool versus UAV threats to K9 artillery
**Date:** 2026-11-16 KST. Scope Hanwha Aerospace investment research only, **NO_BUY_APPROVAL / AERO_ENGINE_IP_OPTION_LONG_TERM / K9_REMAINS_USEFUL_BUT_MUST_EVOLVE**. Separate 2026Q3 operating margin from steady state. Cross-reference earlier HANWHA_GLOBAL_DEFENSE_REARMAMENT_MOAT_20261116.md.

## Primary conclusion
**Own Korean high-bypass/afterburning aviation jet engine IP could deliver exceptionally strong industry economics if military/customer flight-qualified design, engine production and decades-long MRO aftermarket are secured**. Korea advanced precision manufacturing is a cost and supplier ecosystem enabling condition, not a guarantee: high-temperature turbine/superalloy/single-crystal blade processing, turbine cooling, certification, reliability life, full OEM design authority and export controls are current bottlenecks; GE retains F414 design under licensed assembly. Hanwha's 2026 operations are affected by loss-making P&W GTF risk/revenue sharing program.
**FPV ISR/strike drones materially worsen K9 artillery survivability** and shorten firing dwell/force dispersion, but don't make artillery obsolete; drone surveillance teamed with fast artillery fire, suppressive fire, radio-jammed/weather independence and heavier payload remain integral. Independent IISS 2026 shows contract value for NATO Europe self-propelled artillery and MLRS ~**$3B 2018–21 to >$29B 2022–25**, +**867%**. Hanwha K9A2 autoloader 3 crew and K9A3 unmanned/remote controlled concepts respond, **not 2026 universally deployed or mature mass product**. Winning requires counter-UAS, EW, protective systems, precision, move, logistics and service, not old tracked gun without upgrades.

## Global economics of engine IP
AlixPartners 2026 aerospace industry profit pool analysis: major jet engine OEMs collectively **47% of civil aerospace industry 2025 profit pool** and average 19.5% operating margins, driven by long-lived aftermarket economics, vs aircraft manufacturers and structures lower. This 19.5% **NOT a Hanwha margin forecast or peer direct comparator**, and depends on installed fleet, IP rights, MRO, legal liabilities. New engines often initially low/no margin and negative working capital; aftermarket overhaul over 20–40yr lifetimes monetize fleet and locked-in certification/parts. 2026 GE Aerospace official confirms Hanwha **licensed F414 for KF21 and F404 for FA50/T50**, domestic assembly/parts localization, US GE supplies engine kits and retains design IP; no direct full independent OEM royalty capture. High precision Korean manufacturing creates a plausible catch-up but only *design+reliability+scale* yields moat.

## Artillery in drone era
RUSI Oct2025 Watling **"Emergent Approaches to Combined Arms Manoeuvre in Ukraine"** original PDF printed p12 verified using web screenshot zero-index p14. Says howitzers and mortars remain fundamental: low latency, heavier payload, multiple effects, resilient bad-weather and counter EW, but dispersed, camouflaged, drone observation precaution, short bursts often ≤10 rounds, moving positions and UGV replenishment; describes fighting tactics of select successful Ukrainian units, not universal global doctrine. UAV/FPV and **fiber-optic controlled drones immune to radio jamming** heighten exposure. RUSI Justin Bronk August2025 independently argues drones should ENABLE traditional NATO fires not replace them because drone attrition, variable reliability, EW and air defense. This is nuanced, not "UAVs don't matter."
- IISS 2026 independent study **~867% increase in NATO Europe procurement contracts 2018–21 vs 2022–25 across artillery systems (SPHs + MLRS)**, from **over $3B → over $29B**. It reflects Ukraine and stocks replenishment and isn't promised future trend; high procurement *not guaranteed Hanwha share*. NATO Jul7 2026 GENIFR 155mm interoperability initiative and precision ground strike confirm relevance.
- K9 development from Janes: K9A2 3 crew full automatic load, newer 58cal and range improvement, UAS jammer, laser warning, APS intended, tracked IOC hoped 2027 and deliveries future pending; K9A3 concept remote/unmanned, 3 unmanned vehicles 1 control vehicle, claimed >80km intended, no 2026 fielded mass. K9 future upgrade platform may increase equipment/service ASP/market; however nextgen capex and pricing pressure could hurt owner margins.
- Hanwha also owns K239 Chunmoo MLRS/precision rockets business, offering alternative to traditional K9 demand shifts, and export products face KNDS RCH155, BAE Archer, Elbit, future drone-native tech. Counter-UAS may mean new demand outside K9 and Hanwha must capture.
