## C. Verified pushback against a blanket bearish thesis
- **2026 Q3 JBTM adjusted EBITDA margin 18.0% vs 17.1% prior year**, with 2026 FY mid-guide 17.375% and 2028 goal 20%; suggests **actual margin execution progressing** despite sales headwinds.
- Protein Solutions 2026Q3 revenue **+6.3%** and adjusted EBITDA margin **22.4%**; 9mo margin 22.7%, +310bp. This supports food protein automation operating leverage.
- 2026 nine months CFO **$320M**, JBTM-defined FCF $252M, net debt / LTM adjusted EBITDA **2.45x**, so no evidence of corporate cash collapse. GAAP CFO−capex TTM $327.1M across consistent comparison in previous note; share price return still dependent on value paid.
- Barclays bullish perspective 2026-09-08: post-Q2 multiple derating creates attractive risk/reward, historically resilient food-processing equipment, merger creates one of few full-line platforms. Explicitly Barclay's analyst opinion, not fact proven of moat. [S9]
- Thus **Obin's bear case is execution probabilities / valuation**, not independently established argument that AI technology is absent or fish opportunity has no value.
