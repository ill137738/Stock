## 1. National security / "no Chinese cooling gear" claim
**Fact:** trustworthy OT supply chain is economically/security-relevant. Automated data center cooling CDUs, PLCs, BMS/network sensors, remote updates and field service can create confidentiality/availability risks and failures; hyperscalers/government buyers may require stringent security and provenance. DoD classified workloads not equivalent to all commercial hyperscalers.
**Regulatory precision:** Section 889 NDAA (FY2019) restricts **certain specified Chinese telecom and video-surveillance products and companies**, not categorically every Chinese-manufactured passive plate heat exchanger, pump, CDU or chiller. U.S. BIS 2025 regulatory agenda focused on foreign-adversary-controlled **data-center networking equipment/services**, not automatic issued blanket Chinese industrial-cooling prohibition. 2026 GAO highlights covered telecom supplier restrictions and limited deep-tier traceability. Do not claim "all Chinese cooling prohibited" or "all American OEM sourced entirely in USA".
**Counterexample:** Reuters Mar 17, 2026 Google **in talks** with Chinese Envicool and other vendors to buy liquid cooling; not a concluded contract nor authorization for defense/sovereign data centers. Global price competition remains, but security/availability/integration certification can be moat for complex American/Western system OEMs. NVIDIA DSX certification measures ecosystem fit, **not DoD supply security clearance**.

## 4. Does space-based computing work physically? ARK vs BCG *direct primary*
**Engineering:** vacuum eliminates convection with external air, but interior liquid loops can carry heat to ***large radiative heat rejection***; waste power must leave as thermal photons. Low sunlight/eclipse orbit tradeoff, radiation durability, optical inter-satellite links, deployment, replacement cycles matter. NASA proven heat pipes and radiators on small satellites. GAO Apr28 2026 warns large data center power/cooling commercially unproven. BCG estimates **~400 m² of effective radiator for 100kW**, design-dependent: if same radiating temp/emissivity, **1MW ~4,000m²** by linear scaling; larger temperature-radiative Stefan-Boltzmann dependence and orientation may alter drastically. Need lightweight deployed panel and low mass, zero water drain but potentially high launch capex.
- **ARK Jan12 2026:** ***cost parity*** if Starship launch cost reaches ~$**300/kg** vs initial assumptions; 100kW satellite proposed mass **~1,000kg** aspirational and acknowledged unknown.
- **ARK Mar30 2026:** full reusability lowers ~$1,000/kg -> **$100/kg** and model finds ***space compute ~25% cheaper***, partly avoiding grid/permitting time and power/cooling Opex. This is **ARK cost-per-token model conditioned on assumptions**, no demonstrated space DC actual costs; not necessarily "space cooling itself more efficient" thermodynamically.
- **BCG Aug/Sep 2026:** fundamentally different 20-year ***full TCO per MW***. Today space TCO **2.5–3× ground**; achievable realistic 5–10y improvements ~**1.5× ground** with lower launch/weight and 10% failure; exceptionally aggressive **$100/kg launch**, light satellite, only **5% failures** may approach **1.1×** ground, still not 25% cheaper. BCG orbital 10–15% of AI datacenter market by 2040 is market-model ***hypothesis***, not confirmed. Different TCO denominators, failures, hardware lifespan and launch mass yield discrepancy; cannot average them mechanically or call ARK proven wrong/right.
- **Most logical earliest monetization:** process satellite/space-generated imagery near orbit, sovereign batch inference or latency-tolerant compute where uplink/downlink cost/operational specificity justifies premium; interactive real-time low-latency and frontier model huge synchronous training require extraordinary network and reliability before replacing terrestrial DCs.
- **Up-front capital high, cooling hardware entirely different**: even if orbital succeeds, conventional VRT/NVT/MOD cooling pumps and CDU may be relevant as component engineering but their ***orbital revenue currently unproven***. RDW specialized thermal hardware closer to space but lacks earth cash-flow defense. Google Project Suncatcher Oct2026 prototype inference only, not large commercial DC or space radiator TAM proof.

## 5. Technology disruption for Earth cooling: warm-water loops
NVIDIA June21 2026 **Rubin** rack liquid cooling circulating ~**45°C supply /55°C return** permits **dry coolers** rather than compressors/wet cooling towers in suitable climates; this increases demand for cold plates/manifolds/CDUs, piping controls, dry cooler heat rejection but **may reduce conventional chiller dollars per compute MW**. Vendor portfolios exposed unequally: VRT end-to-end trim cooler/dry cooling and liquid chain, NVT rack-side coolant distribution, MOD dry coolers and chillers. High price competition by Envicool and thermal design in-sourcing can compress hardware margins despite strong MW growth. Source: https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/liquid-cooling-ai-factories/
