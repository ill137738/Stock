# MRVL: AI growth valuation, orbital free-space optics, NVIDIA partnership and Lumentum nonlinear synergy

**Research date:** 2026-11-15 KST; US closing stock on 2026-11-13. **Decision:** MRVL strategically more interesting after latest verification, but 'AI will win' is NOT equivalent to $156.76 undervalued; signed merger unclosed, orbital supplier order unverified. No buy/trading approval. Keep VRT, JBTM, PAHC research independent. Sources below primarily company + SEC + NASA + Google independent research + BCG.

## Executive novel facts
1. **NVIDIA direct $2B Marvell strategic investment announced/closed 2026-03-31**, not merely partnership marketing. VRT comparison must account this. NVIDIA and MRVL announce **NVLink Fusion** system compatibility for MRVL custom XPU and NVLink-compatible scale-up networking alongside NVIDIA Vera CPU, ConnectX, BlueField, NVLink, Spectrum-X; separate collaboration on silicon photonics and AI-RAN. SEC MRVL 2026-08-01 10-Q confirms NVIDIA purchased **2,000,000 shares Series A convertible preferred for $2B**, initially convertible max **~21.778M ordinary shares at ~$91.84/share**, subject to filings/conditions; had NOT converted Aug1. For complete pro forma dilutive ownership, add separately on top of acquisition and Google warrants as applicable. $2B equity is **not an annual product order** and ~$91.84 conversion price isn't a stock-floor, deal has preferred rights.
2. **NVIDIA multivendor counterparties** invested **$2B Lumentum 2026-03-02** and **$2B Coherent same date**, with **nonexclusive multibillion laser/optical capacity purchase and future access commitments**. NVIDIA's strategic model is to source multiple suppliers and retain ecosystem decision control, not outsource all network silicon. Cooperation and competition coexist: NVIDIA internal CPO & fabric may bypass portions of merchant DSP, while Marvell supplies custom XPU/PHY/optical IP and supports open user customization.
3. **Orbital AI link:** Google Suncatcher original PDF p.3 screenshot verified. Proposed FSO intersatellite network short-distance *aggregate link target* ~**10Tbps** using commercial **DWDM transceivers**; bench demonstration **800Gbps one-way / 1.6Tbps duplex** (total 2 directions), **NOT flight or 10Tbps delivered**. Existing terminals Mynaric Condor ~2Gbps over very long distances, not matched test; different design goals. 2026 Sep24 Google 2027 2-satellite high-bandwidth laser-link test still planned. MRVL + future LITE high-speed silicon and lasers technically relevant, *no Google Suncatcher MARVELL vendor selection or confirmed contract*. Rocket Lab acquisition Mynaric **closed Apr14 2026**, owns terminal flight optical systems/laser beam pointing/tracking, different skill set than semiconductor SerDes. First product prototypes do not guarantee true flight/mission reliability.
4. **Mass misconception:** Intersatellite link is **free-space beam (no fiber cable, no copper cable)** between separate spacecraft. NASA says laser **optical terminals can have lower size/mass/power vs RF high-bandwidth communications**, not universally lower mass/heat vs every short on-board copper cable. Short on-board copper links can be cost/power superior. Entire laser terminal includes telescopes/apertures, pointing/tracking, stabilization, cooling, rad-hard electronics. Google paper explicitly optimizes compute/kg. BCG 2026 warns orbital infrastructure remains ~2.5–3× terrestrial cost, 100kW orbital heat load could require ~400m² radiator; launch to ~$200/kg mid 2030s is Google hypothetical, not today's delivered launch price. Optical is necessary *candidate bottleneck*, not economic proof for orbital AI.
5. **LITE synergy technically credible (potential 1+1>2):** MRVL DSP/SerDes/TIA/silicon photonics/custom ASIC + LITE InP laser/EML/photodiode/optical circuit switching/photonic manufacturing could vertically co-design, reduce W/bit, packaging, latency, test/qualification cycles and scarce laser supply, and enter near-packaged optics / optical chip-to-chip scale-up where separate vendors could not previously integrate as seamlessly. **But** SEC merger announced Nov2 2026 fixed 4.5 MRVL/LITE share, originally announced LITE $115B enterprise **based on Oct28 unaffected stock prices**, post-close existing MRVL owner **67%**, LITE owner 33%, targeted H1 CY2028, unclosed. Issuer synergy **$1B+ annual run-rate mostly COGS**, issuer nonGAAP EPS accretive first postclosing year forecast, and broad **$550B TAM** *NOT audited realizations or optical product sales*.
6. **New transparent synergy sanity math** on **issuer's Nov2 'most recent quarter annualized' NON-GAAP** numbers, MRVL ~$12.6B sales ×37% adj EBIT margin = $4.662B, LITE ~$5.1B ×42%=$2.142B, combined $6.804B (= ~$6.9B by company rounded 39%×17.7). Old MRVL holders' equivalent 67%×combined adj EBIT without synergy=$4.559B, **~−2.2%** vs premerger MRVL $4.662B. With realized **full $1B pretax synergies**, old MRVL holders share 67%×$7.804B=$5.229B, **~+12.2%** vs prior; neither one-for-one with EPS, especially **not GAAP, no tax, integration, capex, financing, preferred shares, google warrant, stock comp, cash burn or acquired revenue synergies**. 1+1>2 cash result possible but NOT automatically 3x shareholder economics. For delta $0.5B realized synergies old holders share ~$4.894B +5% vs pre; requires execution.
7. **Hidden merger downside:** Merchant DSP chip customers such as InnoLight/Coherent other optical module makers can be Lumentum *downstream competitors* post-merger, may dual-source from **Broadcom**; NVIDIA buys LITE AND COHR—no exclusive moat. Longer working capital/capex to supply InP lasers; more risk in manufacturing. LPO/CPO integration can eliminate traditional pluggable DSP retimers: MRVL future new CPO optical IP has to replace economic value of legacy DSP.
8. **High-valuation vs AI future:** MRVL Nov13 **$156.76**, reported ~$137.63B equity market cap/TTM $9.45B and PSR14.56× (standalone pre-merger historical denominator). Cannot just use $9.45B and compare to combined future revenue or assume 2031 company standalone optimistic $70–90B plan (Oct6 premerger) as guaranteed. Reference after-merger 2026 pro forma *annualized runrate* $17.7B and 39% **adjusted** EBIT, NOT FY2028 audited. **Competing outcomes beyond single optimistic narrative** required.

10. **Investment verdict:** Technical synergy + Nvidia partnership/strategic direct stock stake make Marvell stronger *investment-research candidate* than prior quick comparison recognized, and orbital connectivity is an unpriced **possible** addition, but present $156.76 cannot be certified cheap absent independent transaction close terms, 2027 optical/CPO customer conversion, Lumentum module customer retention, laser capacity returns and normalized full diluted owner FCF. Continue VRT as more realized margin/FCF baseline. The specific technical/valuation circumstances permit MRVL to win over 5–10yr investment but uncertain likelihood; do not change to automatic buy due to acquisition-price fall or NASA satellite optics narrative.
