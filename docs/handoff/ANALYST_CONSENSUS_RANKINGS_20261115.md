## 3. Interpreting expert skill honestly
TipRanks methodology combines success rate, avg return, count/statistical significance; default profitability assessed over 1 year or until analyst updates recommendation. Hold recommendations excluded from profit hit ratio. They are *not* literal accuracy in forecasting the specific 12-month target and no proof of actual trade performance. https://www.tipranks.com/experts/how-experts-ranked

The JPMorgan bullish $55 is by an analyst with a 20% 3/15 overall record, so cannot be treated as stronger than the contradictory Sell.
- PT mean is a reference **not an expected value**. JBTM omitted target by bearish Obin biases price-target sample; PAHC sample is only 2. Provider universe discrepancy is important. Use S&P / FT alongside TipRanks.
- Do not equate low PAHC P/E with strong undervaluation; evaluate working capital, cash conversion, product regulation and control.
- Buying PAHC on catalyst thesis needs real inventory cash release, debt/cash flow, non-antibiotic margins and species-specific aquaculture proof.
- Buying JBTM on engineering moat thesis needs organic book-to-bill/order conversion, 1.8% 9mo organic growth improvement, fish revenue/parts service, integration gains, and value capture.
