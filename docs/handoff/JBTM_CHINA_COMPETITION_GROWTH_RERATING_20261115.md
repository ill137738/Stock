# JBTM China competition / potential growth-stock re-rating (2026-11-15)

**Task:** Stock PM standalone competitive falsification study. **Scope:** JBTM primary; PAHC only comparator. **Status:** `COMPETITOR_RISK_REAL / RELATIVE_PERFORMANCE_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_PROVEN / RE_RATING_UNDERWRITING_HOLD / NO_BUY_APPROVAL`.

## 1. Conclusion
- Strong Chinese price and manufacturing competition is **real**, not hypothetical. JBT Marel's 2025 SEC annual report identifies **Square Technology Group Co Ltd** and **Nantong Freezing Equipment Company Ltd** among its direct/adjacent competitors, alongside BAADER, Meyn, GEA, etc. More competitors in basic slaughter/cooling/packing, potentially moving into precision deboning; the 2025 10-K warns lower-priced competing products may force JBTM price reductions.
- **No credible measured basis** (public independent identical-line controlled ROI or market share by geography) to conclude JBTM is losing China or that it has an unassailable tech lead. Risk is highest in commoditized lines, uncertain in high-yield cutting/deboning/vision and process software integration.
- Chinese incumbent **Square Technology** is a Shanghai-listed industrial freezing equipment producer offering poultry spiral/tunnel freezers, exhibited at IFFA 2025 Frankfurt (established rather than merely a commodity internet listing). Supplier claims international customers Tyson/CP/Hormel/Cargill; do not treat as independently audited contract evidence.
- **Qingdao Raniche Machinery Technology** markets turnkey automated poultry slaughter/evisceration/chilling/cut-up and claims **>10,000 birds/hour** full-line designs and Southeast Asia/Africa/Middle East sales. Listing claims are company marketing and not independent head-to-head proof against Marel's yield, uptime and installed-base support.
- JBT Marel actually **wins high-end Chinese business**: July 7 2026 corporate customer story **Xinsheng Group Boxing plant (Shandong)**, existing relationship since 2011, 2026 planned upgrade **4 ACM-NT cut-up lines + 8 AMF-i deboning systems**; other Xinsheng plants deployed JBT lines, up to 13,500 birds/hour. Proof of real Chinese installed customer order/upgrade, but **one marketed case cannot establish JBT's overall China market share or independent ROI**.
- JBT 2025 10-K details Chinese manufacturing locations/Chinese Marel subsidiaries as well as foreign exposures, so geographic competition includes local sourcing/manufacturing, not purely imports. China's local service can be superior on some projects; JBT global integration/service may be superior for multinationals/export-oriented factories. Cost leadership, supplier financing, tariffs and IP copying risks.
- Key falsification: take competitive bids from same poultry line throughput (birds per hour), capex price, operating labor, saleable yield/quality, expected downtime/warranty, service/spares lead times, financing and incremental cash ROI. If Chinese line matches performance and support at lower TCO, JBT's moat/13% GAAP EBIT case becomes doubtful.
  
## 2. Public equity attention / 'hype'
- Not underfollowed to the point of having nearly zero analysts: **StockAnalysis 9 analysts** average Buy, mean PT ~$160.13, **Yahoo Finance 9 FY2026/2027 EPS estimate contributors**, **TipRanks 6 analysts active last 3 months** (4 Buy, 1 Hold, 1 Sell); these are **different universes/lookbacks**, not an error. TipRanks may show an analyst with no target so average target sample excludes that analyst. Market capitalization dated **2026-11-13 ~US$6.41bn**, NYSE and Nasdaq Iceland. Important public industrial equipment/security but not mega-cap tech/high-attention AI pure-play.
- Last Nov 13 close **$125.54**, company TTM GAAP P/E **~33.5x**, forward **normalized/adjusted** P/E **~13.7–15.2x** depending FY2027 analyst consensus vs FY2026 company guidance (different denominator). **Do not claim totally undiscovered deep value**: GAAP earnings multiple includes acquisition accounting, adjusted EPS high due amortization/exclusions.
- 2026 9mo reported revenue +4.3% but organic **+1.8%**; quarterly Q3 YoY group **−0.7%**. Company 2025–28 target organic revenue CAGR 5–7%, adjusted EBITDA margin 20% by 2028 (NOT GAAP EBIT). EPS profit-growth from one-time merger synergy and buybacks can occur without durable top-line growth.
- **"Growth stock re-rating" conditions**: multi-quarter recurring >5–7% *organic* bookings/revenue with transparent FX; convincing actual 1st/2nd tier SKU tech share gains in China/emerging markets vs Chinese quotes; recurring service/software segment profit + verified AI monetization; sustainable GAAP EBIT, ROIC & FCF post synergy; investor awareness via guidance/history/IR reviews. Marketing narrative and analyst coverage can amplify price movement but do not generate durable cashflow.
- **No promise of multiple expansion:** If FY2027 *normalized adjusted* consensus EPS **$9.36** (dynamic Yahoo Finance) then price-to-earnings scenario **11× => ~$103**, **13.4× => ~$125**, **18× => ~$168.5**, with same hypothetical EPS; not a target price, ignores future years and mistakes about GAAP/adjustments. A change in earnings multiple may exceed growth rate for short periods, but a reversal is equally possible.
- An AI/automation 'hype' can catalyze short-term rerating, **not a necessary or sufficient condition for an investable growth company**. Industrial companies can compound intrinsic value through EPS/FCF growth with stable valuation multiples; if organic growth stays 1–3% and recurring AI profit unproven, hype may create overvaluation and later correction.
- PM judgment: **China competitive pressure modest-to-severe by process**, deserves increased underwriting skepticism of 10%+ company organic sales CAGR and 13–14% sustained GAAP EBIT assumptions. High-end switching costs and real China orders support JBT differentiation, but low-cost catch-up/segment mix prevents treating it as durable monopoly. In current state quality industrial-growth/GARP candidate, not proven AI hypergrowth leader. Research priority JBTM retained, PAHC as alternative value.
