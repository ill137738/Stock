# VRT vs NVT — Orbital DC optionality and terrestrial AI data-center exposure (2026-11-15)
**Status:** `RESEARCH_COMPLETED / ORBITAL_SUPPLY_UNVERIFIED / NO_BUY_APPROVAL`. Prior AI_COOLING_SCREEN_R0 research remains distinct from JBTM/PAHC workstreams. All valuation quoted US close **2026-11-13**. Figures include FY2025 end-market exposure and 2026 Q3 / FY26 outlook as labeled; do not silently equate dates.

## Executive answer
VRT offers substantially greater **existing terrestrial data center infrastructure exposure** and a fuller physical **power-and-thermal chain**, larger support network and reported leadership in broad thermal management, **but no verified 2026 commercial orbital AI data-center cooling or radiator contracts**. NVT offers specialized rack-level thermal/power systems and AMD Helios product qualification, but 2025 ~25% revenue datacenter vs VRT 85%, with material utility/industrial/commercial diversification. **Better terrestrial business fit does not imply better expected stock returns**, VRT FY2026 guided *adjusted EPS* P/E ~28.3× at 11/13 vs NVT ~21.3×; GAAP trailing P/E ~37.8× vs 27.7×; VRT 2026 Q3 GAAP EBIT margin 22.8%, NVT 18.6%. 2026 **FULL YEAR organic revenue guidance VRT 37–39%, NVT 38–39% (similar!)**, despite Q3 55% VRT vs 44% NVT. Don't misstate VRT universally faster by guidance.

## Terrestrial exposure and service moat (FY2025 official)
- **Vertiv 2026 company fact sheet** FY2025 revenue about $10.23B, **85% data centers, 10% communication networks, 5% commercial/industrial**. By broad categories this implies ~$8.7B FY2025 data-center-related sales. **20% services and spares / 80% products**. **~5,000 global field service engineers / ~320 centers / 130+ countries**, ~34k employees. Vertiv claims **'#1 in thermal management' citing Dell'Oro 2025 DC Physical Infrastructure report**. Caveat: *data center thermal management broad category*, not proof #1 market share in specifically direct-to-chip cold plates, cooling CDUs, spacecraft radiators, or even all cooling definitions. Supplier's citation not independent access to Dell'Oro paywalled rankings.
- **nVent March 18 2026 Investor Day and 2025 results:** FY2025 total revenue ~$3.9B, **data center ~$1.0B (about 25.6% of sales)**; overall Infrastructure vertical **~45%** includes *power utilities as well as data centers* and should not be treated as 45% data-center-only. ~75% of **nVent data-center sales** are 'white space' and 25% 'gray space', not percentages of consolidated revenue. nVent $1M/MW **served value proposition** is company estimation, not measured market share. NVT divested legacy Thermal Management business Jan 2025 (industrial heat tracing), while building Data Solutions liquid cooling; divestiture doesn't mean NVT left AI cooling market.
- nVent operates cooling CDUs, racks, manifolds, rear-door heat exchangers, cable management, power connections, smart power and electrical protection. Opened Blaine MN manufacturing and announced Jul 2026 a further 160k sqft expansion (400k sqft cooling capacity build in 3 years), plus acquisition Maverick Power Oct 1 2026. Strong, credible AMD Helios collaboration announced **October 13 2026**: integrated **DC-powered rear-door heat exchanger** for AMD Helios, handles **residual rack heat after direct-to-chip**. This is specific component integration, not an exclusive all-Helios contract or sole supplier of Helios cooling.
- Vertiv supplies air and liquid cooling CDUs, chilled-water systems, outside heat rejection, UPS switchgear power distribution, integrated power/cooling factory modules, and a very wide long-term service network. **2.3MW CoolChip CDU qualified NVIDIA DSX Ready Sept 21 2026** (self-qualification functional criteria; doesn't override site engineering or imply sole supplier/exclusivity). 2026 company strategy collaborations NVIDIA/Dell/Caterpillar/Generate, supporting integrated power & thermal. VRT has structural advantage selling/servicing entire physical infrastructure for high-uptime AI deployments; NVT has credible advantages in individual components/racks and related adjacent power utility verticals.

## Orbital thermal technology: transferred competencies vs missing ones
Two linked but unequal layers:
1. **Chip heat acquisition/transmission (some transferable know-how)**: cold plates, controlled pumps, liquid loops, CDUs, heat exchangers, monitoring, redundancy; VRT and NVT are both capable terrestrial suppliers in broad sense; no proof existing products already space-qualified.
2. **Final radiative rejection in vacuum (not demonstrated by VRT/NVT)**: heat pipes/two-phase loops proven in space regime, huge deployable mass/area-optimized radiators with high emissivity, space radiation tolerance, micron impacts, launch shock/vibration, microgravity behavior, sun/Earth heating, long on-orbit no-maintenance lifecycle.
**GAO April 28 2026** states orbital data centers at large power/cooling scale UNPROVEN. **BCG Aug 2026** illustrates ~400m² radiator for 100kW and possible 5–10yr to significant technical scale, often higher cost than Earth; calculations model-dependent. NASA 2026 thermal review showcases flight-qualified dedicated space thermals **Redwire Q-Rad** etc but these are not verified AI MW radiators.
- Searches of VRT/NVT public filings/news by 2026-11-15 did **not verify** either supplying orbital AI computer thermal radiators or booking related revenue. Label **UNKNOWN / not found, do not assert company categorically incapable**.
- If orbital compute becomes a large market, VRT may enter via R&D, OEM consortium, acquisition/licensing or cooling-loops; NVT likewise. However the value/cost bottleneck may accrue to *space-specific radiators rather than on-Earth CDUs*, so extrapolating AI terrestrial multiple to orbit is unjustified.
- Space is more likely to **complement terrestrial for in-orbit edge processing** than replace proximity-sensitive inference / general-purpose computing. If orbital costs dramatically decrease, off-Earth shift could eventually reduce some terrestrial capital demand and VRT's existing terrestrial customer spend; optionality isn't uniformly bullish for VRT.

## PM verdict and evidence gates
- **If thesis is data center capex capture and full systems integration**: VRT stronger *business fit* given 85% DC, Dell'Oro broad thermal leadership as cited, larger technical service and higher recent GAAP op margins. Genuine downside: P/E and hyperscaler customer/capex concentration.
- **If thesis is exposure at cheaper valuation and diversification**: NVT credible alternative (AMD Helios/expanded US cooling production), ~$1B 2025 DC sales yet lower company-wide DC reliance, and FY2026 organic guidance not inferior.
- **If thesis is only orbital data center growth**: neither VRT nor NVT can currently be labeled as a **verified pure-play/beneficiary**. Dedicated space companies RDW & others perhaps have closer tech, but no verified MW-scale contracts. Do not impute space TAM to either valuation.
- Future research: company's **cooling-only** revenue and gross profit (VRT 85% is entire DC power+cooling; NVT 25% includes DC electrical power), customer concentration, orders timing and cancellation, organic orders multiple Qs, contract-based NVT Helios revenue, VRT DSX shipments, official 2027+ normalized EPS & FCF, independent performance comparisons/market share, and explicit orbital radiator/loop space-qualification contract evidence.
