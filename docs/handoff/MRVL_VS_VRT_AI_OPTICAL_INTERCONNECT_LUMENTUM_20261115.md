# Stock PM — Marvell (MRVL) vs Vertiv (VRT): optical AI interconnect versus DC cooling (2026-11-15)

This is a distinct AI_INDUSTRIAL_VS_SEMICONDUCTOR comparative study; retain prior JBTM/PAHC and VRT/NVT work without changing approved allocations. All USD stock prices are **2026-11-13 US close**; Marvell and Vertiv quarters are different fiscal/calendar periods. Main conclusion: Marvell's optical connectivity technological barriers look potentially stronger than generic CDU hardware, and Gartner explicitly names Marvell AI-DC optical connectivity 'Company to Beat,' but latest Marvell–Lumentum all-stock mega-merger and very high sales multiple make it **more speculative as a stock**; Vertiv offers already-realized high GAAP margins/FCF and no equally enormous pending stock-swap M&A. **Neither automatically a buy.**

## What each firm actually sells
- **VRT** thermal/cooling CDUs, heat exchangers, cold plates, outside dry coolers/chillers, racks, UPS/switchgear/power conversion, prefabricated systems and global after-service (2025 ~85% consolidated sales to data center end market). Value to buyer: physical uptime, fast build/commissioning, one accountable partner, total cost of operation. Direct strong competitors Schneider/Motivair, Johnson Controls, Eaton, CoolIT, nVent, and Chinese Envicool for generic cooling; barrier in high-MW integration/support, **not exclusive CDU design**.
- **MRVL** electrical/optical high-speed PHY/DSPs/SerDes (optical pluggables), switching and storage silicon, custom ASIC/XPU (Google TPU supporting ecosystem) and future scale-up Photonic Fabric; **not 'pure optical module manufacturer'** and much of DC growth and projected FY31 is custom AI silicon. Latest FY27 **Q2 ending Aug 1 2026** data center **$2.1715B / $2.7393B =79.3%** of quarterly group, DC revenue **+46% YoY**, full company **+37%**, vs VRT **FY26 Q3 ended Sep 30** group **+61% reported, +55% organic**. Gartner's July 16 2026 'Company to Beat' ranking explicitly **Marvell optical connectivity**, **Broadcom custom AI silicon**, **NVIDIA AI network fabric**; Gartner also warns optical CPO execution, hyperscaler vertical integration and competing optical architectures. 'MRVL is NVIDIA of all networking' unsupported.
- Marvell Q2 FY27 **GAAP gross margin 53.1%, op margin ~16.8% ($459.7M / $2.7393B)**, EPS GAAP **$0.33** vs **non-GAAP $0.94** (large gap from SBC, acquisition amortization, items). - Celestial AI acquisition: first expected contribution second half FY28, exit FY28 Q4 **$500M annualized run rate**, exit FY29 Q4 **$1B annualized**; expected, unverified; once LITE mega-merger incorporated FY31 no longer standalone target automatically.
- Merger risk severe even when volume AI optical grows; anti-trust/foreign reviews, culture manufacturing integration, stock swap fixed vs changing price, synergy delayed, future SBC / pro forma dilution; Google $120B conditional warrant cannot be mistaken for unconditional purchase agreement.
- Gartner: custom ASIC chief competitor is **Broadcom**, not VRT; optical DSP/copper/fiber segments also compete with NVIDIA, Credo, Astera Labs, integrated hyperscaler R&D and LITE's competing customer relationships. Photonics market may grow faster than DC HVAC, but CPO can disrupt **incumbent pluggable optical DSP** spend and merchant markets; Marvell needs successful transition. Some GPU links can remain on low-cost copper at shorter reach; not all optical links can be replaced with fibers and delivered via MRVL.
- Orbital data centers: both VRT (vacuum radiators) and MRVL (orbital hardened photonics/optical comms) currently **have no independently verified commercial orbital AI data center thermal / photonic sales**; don't assign orbital TAM to either.

## Decision framework
**What user is actually purchasing:** AI growth plus *durable moat* without overpaying, not ARK-like thematic hype.
- If prioritizing **visible margins, FCF, reliable AI deployment for existing terrestrial DC**, keep **VRT stronger investable quality baseline**; 2026 strong profit history, services/cold loop integrated support, forward adjusted FCF ~3.5%. Still pricey growth and cyclical AI CAPEX.
- If prioritizing **more IP differentiation, possible dramatically stronger 2030+ growth**, **MRVL potentially higher upside**, supported by Gartner optical leadership + hyperscaler design wins; however >$115B priced mega-merger, Google potential share warrant and far more uncertain owner earnings make **risk significantly higher**. The 2026 drawdown doesn't establish margin of safety. As of Nov15 **do not automatically switch VRT to MRVL**.
- Research priorities before turning this into a stock recommendation: **pro forma 2028–31 MRVL+LITE GAAP EBIT and FCF per fully diluted share**, independent downside to 'synergies >$1B' and combined margins, merger close/abort sensitivity, Google warrant conditional vesting and potential customer concentration, MRVL long-run optical vs custom standalone revenue/margins, Broadcom competitive customer overlap, vs VRT normalized 2027–30 thermal backlog/GAAP owner FCF at same macro hypotheses.

## Update 2026-11-15: Does orbital AI computing necessarily use Marvell photonics?
**Owner hypothesis:** intersatellite high-bandwidth optics are needed in orbital AI clusters, therefore MRVL should participate. **PM verdict: strongly relevant candidate, NOT proven vendor or inevitable winner. Assign signed orbital AI compute revenue = $0 in base-case until order/contract named.**

**Primary design evidence:** Google Project Suncatcher research paper (2025) architecture proposes dozens of TPUs per satellite, short-range free-space intersatellite optical links; **~10 Tbps aggregate bandwidth per link is an engineering design goal**, inspired by commercial-off-the-shelf **DWDM** optical transceivers from terrestrial data center applications. Google reports **800 Gbps one-way / 1.6 Tbps full-duplex bench demonstration on ground**, NOT 10Tbps orbit link. Sep 24 2026 Google statement reiterates **2027 2-satellite laser-link on-orbit test scheduled**, not completed. 2026 first prototype/TPU inference does not itself demonstrate multi-satellite optical 10Tbps.

**MRVL technical overlap:** official FY2026 10-K lists optical PAM4 DSP (up to 1.6T), coherent/coherent-lite transceivers, silicon photonics, TIA/laser drivers, SerDes, CPO and custom accelerators, plus custom ASIC products for *aerospace and defense*. Celestial AI Photonic Fabric scale-up acquired Feb 2 2026. Thus multiple relevant technology components could be adapted to free-space optics and in-satellite cluster. These are **commercial terrestrial IP capability claims and relevant design domains, not spaceflight radiation-qualified operating status**, no Google Project Suncatcher Marvell selected supplier. Existing Google custom TPU agreement cannot be attributed to Google's orbital roadmap without a distinct public statement.
**Different products:** (1) intra-satellite chip/rack optical connectivity may reuse high speed DSP/photonic components, (2) inter-satellite free-space optical *terminal* needs optical telescope, high accuracy beam pointing and tracking, vibration, thermal vac, radiation tolerance plus modem/DSP, (3) downlink to ground with atmosphere issues, (4) internal short electrical links not automatically optical. Flight-qualified terminals competitive advantage may accrue to dedicated primes.
**Crucial competitor updated:** Rocket Lab (RKLB) **completed acquisition of Mynaric on Apr 14, 2026**, now owns CONDOR optical inter-satellite terminals. Real product technical claims are ~2.5 Gbps at long range (different architecture from planned 10 Tbps short-range Suncatcher links). NASA lasercomm vendor lists TESAT, CACI, SpaceX, L3 etc. Rocket Lab satellite terminal experience is closer to flight-ready space hardware than MRVL, but its deployed rate is far below proposed ultra-high-rate near-neighbor optical computing. Do not assume either wins Suncatcher.
**Risk:** vacuum optics laser power budget, radiation hardening, pointing precision, thermal stability, flight reliability, launch cost, cost/watt economics and actual customer sourcing are unverified; orbital DC market may not be economic vs Earth. MRVL stock already priced for ambitious terrestrial 2031 targets and signed Lumentum merger with 33% pro forma stake to LITE holders, so orbital optionality must not justify additional premium based only on thematic fit.
