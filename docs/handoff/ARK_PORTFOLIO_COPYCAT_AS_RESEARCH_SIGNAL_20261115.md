## What its holdings actually signal
- ARK publishes **end-of-day holdings daily** and optional daily trade notice emails, not simply stale 13Fs. ARK states emails omit ETF share creation/redemption and IPOs, upload after close, don't identify exact execution times, unofficial/unreconciled pending NAV/custodian records. Daily changes may be *risk management / price appreciation / allocations / client flows*, not necessarily new high conviction, nor disclosed exact paid price. https://www.ark-funds.com/ark-trade-notifications
- SEC 13F is due within **45 days** of quarter-end, so using 13F as main signal loses timeliness even versus ARK holdings updates. https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f
- ARKK holdings concentrated (2026-11-12 StockAnalysis **43 holdings, 51.25% in top 10**, Tesla 11.37%, Space Exploration 7.66%, Tempus 6.27%, Circle 4.34%, CRISPR 4.14%), so a strategy copying a few top stocks can be even more concentrated and riskier. Holdings differ between aggregators by cutoff time and security classification; use official daily CSV over compiled screenshots. https://stockanalysis.com/etf/arkk/holdings/
- Active ARKK management **0.75%/yr** fee (ARK official). Buying individual stock doesn't incur this exact fund fee but incurs own transaction/tax/execution and research costs. https://helpcenter.ark-funds.com/what-is-arks-management-fee-expense-ratio
- ARK's *ARKQ* and *ARKX* holdings represent robotics and space/defense narratives, **not pure cooling or proof of orbital heat-rejection contracts**. Nov 13 ARKQ top funds Tesla, Space Exploration, Nvidia, Kratos, Rocket Lab, Alphabet, AMD, etc; ARKX top Space Exploration, L3Harris, Rocket Lab; only top-list verified. No assertion that VRT/NVT absent entire portfolio based on top lists. https://www.ark-funds.com/funds/ARKX ; https://stockanalysis.com/etf/arkq/holdings/
- Big Ideas 2026 is a **forward-looking internal hypothesis report**. Distinguish total addressable market (TAM), adoption S-curves, required capital expenditure, current revenue, profitability, FCF, firm market share, price already implied in EV/FCF. ARK research can be optimistic by design and risk-adjusted odds cannot be inferred just from projections. https://www.ark-invest.com/big-ideas-2026

## Specific approach for the Owner's AI DC cooling investment thesis (VRT vs NVT vs MOD vs RDW)
Do not elevate or disqualify VRT/NVT solely for presence/absence in ARKK, ARKQ, ARKX: ARK's mandate and cap constraints are different. Treat ARK space themes (launch cost improvements, power supply, or compute potential) as *scenarios* requiring NASA/GAO thermal proof; current **VRT/NVT orbital AI data-center sales UNCONFIRMED**, while terrestrial already generates operational revenues.
**ARK-gated research workflow**:
1. Identify new industry/subtheme from ARK Big Ideas or notable new consistent allocations in ARKK, ARKQ, ARKX; use time-stamped **official holdings**. Never chase a one-day trade alert.
2. Separate **industry tailwind** from **listed company earnings exposure**. For VRT: DC 85% FY2025, 2026Q3 group organic +55%, GAAP EBIT 22.8%, focus on cooling-only revenue and orders; for NVT FY25 DC ~25%, 2026Q3 group organic +44%, GAAP op 18.6%, lower FY2026 adjusted PE, avoid cooling segment conflation.
3. Verify customer orders, recurring service gross margins, AI cooling moat, ROIC and capex/FCF as reported by company and SEC; compare competitor China pricing and product.
4. **Independent valuation:** reverse engineer 3/5/10-year growth required by today's market cap and FCF yield; use range of EV/EBIT or owner cash flow vs peers. Stress hyperscaler capex slowdown and terminal multiple contraction.
5. Maintain own watchlist with **predefined buy price / rejection criteria** independent of ARK buys/sells; avoid FOMO. More than one quarter/few disclosures confirmation as appropriate; ARK profit-taking or rebalancing is not automatically bearish.
6. Track performance of **Owner's ex ante stock picks** vs relevant index or diversified benchmark with transaction costs and risk, not narrative-only agreement with ARK.
