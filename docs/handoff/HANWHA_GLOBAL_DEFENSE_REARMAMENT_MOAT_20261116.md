## PM opinion
**Defense sector > structural growth likely** due already launched multi-year government procurement regardless new war. **Hanwha can win in tracked self-propelled artillery, guided rockets, integrated long-range strike** based actually bought NATO exports, price and delivery & Romania/Poland localization; has stronger evidence than mere concept stock. **Not guaranteed winner in all defense segments** against Rheinmetall/KNDS/BAE/RTX/Elbit and startup drone/missile tech. Major moat 5yr is industrial scale, interoperability, shipment reliability, future service & training, not indefinite monopsony; growth can create manufacturing profit but capex and local content dilute shareholder economics. Evaluate as strong **globally investable growth candidate**, **not global fixed #2**, buy judgment requires FY26Q4 Land EBIT mix, 2027 shareholder FCF, US Army production award, cost/sourcing in EU, signed orders.
